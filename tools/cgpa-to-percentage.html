Percentage to CGPA

How CGPA is converted to percentage

The most common formula is Percentage = CGPA × 9.5. It is used by CBSE and by many colleges and universities in India. For example, a CGPA of 8.5 gives 8.5 × 9.5 = 80.75%.

To convert back, use CGPA = Percentage ÷ 9.5.

Not every institution uses 9.5. Some universities use a multiplier of 10, and others have their own conversion tables. If you need the result for an official form, check your university's rules or ask your college office. You can change the multiplier above to match.
