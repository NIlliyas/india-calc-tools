Simple and Compound Interest Calculator

How interest is calculated

Simple interest = P × R × T ÷ 100, where P is the principal, R is the yearly rate and T is the time in years. Interest is earned only on the original amount.

Compound interest = P × (1 + R ÷ (100 × n))n × T − P, where n is how many times interest is added each year. Interest is earned on the principal and on the interest already added, so the amount grows faster over time.

The longer the time and the more often interest is compounded, the bigger the difference between the two. Banks and lenders may use different rules and rounding, so treat the result as an estimate. Your numbers are processed in your browser and are not stored.
