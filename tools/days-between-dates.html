Days Between Two Dates Calculator

How the days between two dates are counted

The calculator counts the number of days from the start date up to, but not including, the end date. For example, from 1 January to 2 January is 1 day. If you also want to count the end date, add 1 day to the result.

The "weekdays" figure counts Monday to Friday only. It does not remove public holidays, which differ by state and year. The months and days breakdown counts complete calendar months first, then the remaining days.

This is useful for exam countdowns, notice periods, project deadlines, loan or leave planning, and event dates. If you enter the dates in reverse order, the calculator swaps them for you. Your dates are processed in your browser and are not stored.
