How the discount is calculated

Price after discount = original price × (1 − discount ÷ 100). For example, 25% off ₹2,000 = ₹2,000 × 0.75 = ₹1,500.

If there is an extra discount, it is applied on the already reduced price, not on the original. So 25% off followed by an extra 10% off is not the same as 35% off. In the example, ₹1,500 × 0.90 = ₹1,350, which is a total saving of 32.5%.

Some shops apply extra discounts differently, so check the final bill. Your numbers are processed in your browser and are not stored.
