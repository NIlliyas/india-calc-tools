Age Calculator

How age is calculated

Your age is the time between your date of birth and the "age as on" date. The calculator counts complete years first, then the remaining complete months, then the remaining days.

This is useful for school admissions, exam and job eligibility, and government forms that ask for your age on a specific date. Change the "age as on" date to check your age on the last date of an application. Your dates are processed in your browser and are not stored.
