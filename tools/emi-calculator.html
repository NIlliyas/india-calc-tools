How EMI is calculated

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Instalment, the fixed amount you pay your bank every month until the loan is cleared. It is calculated with this formula:

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1)

Here P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate (yearly rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100), and n is the number of monthly instalments. A longer tenure gives a lower EMI but a higher total interest. This calculator gives an estimate, and your bank's final figure may differ slightly because of fees and rounding.
