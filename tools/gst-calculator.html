How GST is calculated

Adding GST: GST amount = price × rate ÷ 100, and final price = price + GST amount.

Removing GST: original price = GST-inclusive price × 100 ÷ (100 + rate), and GST amount = inclusive price − original price.

For sales within the same state, GST is split equally into CGST and SGST. For sales between states, the full amount is charged as IGST. GST rates can change, so please check the rate for your product or service on the official GST website before filing or billing.
