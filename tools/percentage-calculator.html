How percentages are calculated

X% of Y = Y × X ÷ 100. For example, 20% of 500 = 500 × 20 ÷ 100 = 100.

X is what percent of Y = X ÷ Y × 100. For example, 50 out of 200 = 50 ÷ 200 × 100 = 25%.

Percentage change = (new value − old value) ÷ old value × 100. A positive result is an increase and a negative result is a decrease.

These are useful for exam marks, discounts, price changes, and profit or loss. Your numbers are processed in your browser and are not stored.
